Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Injured Soccer Players (injuredsoccerplayers.com) provides automated injury monitoring and player durability analytics. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent data collection practices.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to analyze general site traffic, user interactions, and performance metrics. GA4 collects anonymized data including your approximate geographic location, browser type, device specifications, and page interaction events via cookies. No personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers is collected by this service.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Third-Party Advertising

We may display advertising via third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense). These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You may manage personalized advertising settings at Google Ad Settings.

4. Data Sources & Disclaimer

All athlete injury telemetry, availability statuses, and durability timelines are aggregated from publicly available football reporting and data providers. This tool is designed for statistical analysis and fantasy sports evaluation. We do not provide clinical or medical diagnoses.

5. Contact

For questions or feedback regarding this policy, visit our project repository on GitHub.
